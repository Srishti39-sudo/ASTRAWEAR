# Design the Winged ASTRA Logo (myself)

Tags: #brand #design
Goal: **ASTRA** wordmark with **wings** — old money, yet cool. One color, minimal, ownable.
Related: [[Brand Identity]] · [[Figma — Build the ASTRA Logo]]

## Tool
- **Canva** (fastest) or **Figma** (vector, better for a forever logo). Either is free.

## Font — "old money yet cool" (pick one, all free on Google Fonts)
- **Cinzel** — Roman inscriptional caps. The most "old money cool." ⭐ try first
- **Marcellus** — refined engraved caps (Hermès family)
- **Italiana** — thin, editorial, fashion
- **Cormorant Garamond** — soft, timeless
- Type `ASTRA` in caps, **letter-spacing wide** (~0.3em). That spacing is what reads luxury.

## Add the wings
- Use **two wings from your swan/dove mark**, mirrored, spreading from behind or beside the word — like the word is taking flight.
- Placement options: wings **behind/under** ASTRA (rising up), or a small winged mark **above** the word.
- Keep them **fine-line, one color** (near-black `#33302A`). Thin and elegant > detailed and busy.
- ⚠️ Draw your own wings (or trace lightly) so you own the artwork for trademark.

## Export & hand it to me
1. Background: **transparent**.
2. Export **PNG @2x** (and **SVG** if Figma).
3. Save into: `ASTRAWEAR/frontend/images/`  (make the folder)
4. Name it: `logo.png` (and `logo.svg` if you have it).
5. Tell me: *"logo is in frontend/images"* → I'll drop it into the top bar in place of the text ASTRA, sized and centered, with the text as a fallback.
