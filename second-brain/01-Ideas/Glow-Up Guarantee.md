# Glow-Up Guarantee

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #experience #promise

## The idea
The glow-up isn't implied — it's **visible and guaranteed**. Every result shows "you now" → "you styled," a real before/after, plus a taste/confidence level that climbs as you use ASTRA. The transformation is the product, made undeniable.

> The promise: "yes, the glow-up is real — and here's the proof."

## Why it matters
- Turns an abstract promise into a felt, screenshot-able moment — which is also the **most shareable** thing you can make (fuels the reels).
- **Peak-End Rule**: the reveal is the emotional peak; a before/after ending sends people out on a high. That's what they remember and retell.
- A rising level gives a sense of progress → reason to return (goal-gradient + streak behaviour).

## How it could work
1. Always render the pair: uploaded photo → styled avatar, side by side.
2. A subtle "glow-up / taste level" that increases with looks tried, saved, worn.
3. Shareable card export (on-brand sand + sea) for the before/after — built-in virality.
4. Milestones: "you've unlocked your palette," "you know your silhouette."

## Open questions
- How to show before/after tastefully (never make the "before" feel like an insult).
- Is the level public/shareable or private?
