# Personal Stylist — The Returning Loop

Status: core bet · Tags: #idea #personal-stylist #retention #north-star

> **Create Avatar & Astro tell her who she is (one-time). Personal Stylist is why she comes back (daily).** This is the retention engine — the actual business.

## The validation (already proven)
I already use this — by hand, inside ChatGPT. I pasted a product (Mandarin Collar Halter Crop Top), typed my measurements (36 shoulders, 34 bust, 27 upper / 30 lower waist), and asked *"is this good for me?"* and *"is it giving European vibes?"* — and got a real, useful answer: does it suit my proportions, the vibe, a rating /10, what to pair it with, the one caution.

**That behaviour IS the demand.** I'm hacking it together myself because I need it. Strongest possible signal.

## The two real pains
1. **"What do I wear?"** — Yesterday I had to go out and didn't know what to wear; I tried on everything.
2. **"What do I buy?"** — Shopping online, I don't know if something will suit me before buying.

## The three jobs (in build order)
1. **"Should I buy this?"** (build first) — paste a link/screenshot → ASTRA already knows my body + essence → tells me if it suits me, the vibe, a rating /10, what to pair it with, the one caution. **No image generation needed.** This is exactly my ChatGPT convo, made automatic + personal.
2. **"Dress me from what I own"** — my wardrobe photos + my body → outfit combos, so I don't try on everything. See [[Wardrobe Memory]].
3. **"Show me *wearing* it" + better options, same price** — virtual try-on image + shopping agent. See [[Shop-Anywhere Companion]] · [[Best-Price Finder]]. **After funding.**

## Honest ratings
- **Demand:** 9.5/10 — proven by my own behaviour, daily frequency, real pain.
- **Job 1 (advice stylist):** 9/10 — buildable NOW. Same reasoning as Create Avatar/Astro, pointed at a product. The ChatGPT convo had *zero images* and was still useful → I can ship the loop without the hard tech.
- **Job 3 (virtual try-on image):** desire 10/10, today's tech ~4–5/10. True identity-preserving try-on (my face + body + the real garment) is the hardest thing in fashion AI. Reach for it later, don't gate launch on it.
- **Job 2 (wardrobe):** 7/10, strong retention, medium effort. Phase 2.
- **Overall:** 9/10, unusually de-risked — the best version needs none of the hard tech.

## The moat vs just using ChatGPT
In ChatGPT I had to **re-type my measurements every time.** ASTRA already *knows* me — body (Create Avatar) + essence (Astro). So the stylist is one paste away, always personal, in my warm voice. **ChatGPT is a blank stranger every time; ASTRA remembers.** That memory is the moat.

## v1 to build (shippable)
**"Should I buy this?"** — paste a link or a screenshot of anything I'm eyeing. ASTRA answers instantly using my saved profile: *does it suit me · the vibe · a rating /10 · what to pair it with · the one caution.* Warm, personal, beautiful. Then layer "dress me from my closet," then try-on imagery.
