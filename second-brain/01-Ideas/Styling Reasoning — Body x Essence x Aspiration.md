# Styling Reasoning — Body × Essence × Aspiration

Status: core principle · Tags: #principle #styling #prompt

## The principle
Every ASTRA recommendation must answer **two things at once**, and name both:
1. **What it does for the body** (defines your waist, gives structure, shows your curves, elongates your legs).
2. **What it says about who you are + who you're becoming** (your calm, your whimsy, your founder authority).

The **fabric, the colour, and the cut** each get chosen for a reason that bridges the two.

> "A light satin midi — because it *defines your waist* (body) and moves with your *calm, whimsical* energy (essence)."
> "A structured polyester jacket — because it gives you the *architecture* your founder self wants (aspiration) while *showing off your curves* (body)."

## The two layers (from the founder)
- **Layer 1 — base:** her body type / silhouette. The anchor. Like "I'm calm."
- **Layer 2 — aspiration:** who she wants to become (founder, model). The direction. Like "becoming a founder."
- The bridge: her base naturally *carries* her toward the aspiration — "I'm calm, so founder is natural for me." Styling shows how her real body supports the image she's reaching for.

## Why it matters
- This is the "she *knows* me" layer. Competitors stop at "flatters your shape." ASTRA adds *why it's you*.
- Fabric carries meaning: satin's softness = calm; polyester/structure = authority. Colour and cut too.
- It's the reasoning engine behind the cards' "why", the 3 statement looks, and the whole results phase.

## Where it plugs in
- The two-layer **questions** (base essence + aspiration) feed this.
- Every card "why" and every look justification uses body + essence + aspiration.
