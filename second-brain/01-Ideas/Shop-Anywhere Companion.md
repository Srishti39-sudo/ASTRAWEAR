# Shop-Anywhere Companion

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #money #moat #shopping

## The idea
A personal stylist in your pocket **while you shop — anywhere**. Browsing any site or standing in a store, ASTRA tells you *yes, this fits your silhouette + vibe + palette + what you already own* — or *skip it*. And it **keeps the record**: every purchase goes into your wardrobe, so it never lets you buy an orphan again.

> Solves the handwritten pain directly: "what I order doesn't go with anything."

## Why it matters
- This is where **the moat and the money meet**:
  - **Money** — affiliate commission when she buys through ASTRA (see [[Affiliate Marketplace]]).
  - **Moat** — her real wardrobe + purchase history is data no retailer can copy. The longer she uses it, the more it knows, the less she'd leave.
- Stops bad purchases *before* they happen — the highest-value moment to be present is at the point of buying.
- Indyx has an "in-store checker," so this must be **more personal** (body + palette + astro + owned wardrobe), not just a fit score.

## How it could work
1. **Browser extension / share-sheet**: paste or share a product link → instant "suits you?" verdict + why, + how to wear it with pieces you own.
2. **In-store**: snap the item or scan the tag → same verdict.
3. Every "yes / bought" logs into [[Wardrobe Memory]].
4. Recs pull from the [[Fashion Clothes Bank]] + live affiliate feeds.
