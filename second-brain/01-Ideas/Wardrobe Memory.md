# Wardrobe Memory

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #data #retention

## The idea
ASTRA remembers **everything you own**. Your real closet lives in the app, so every new suggestion works with pieces you already have. "Nothing goes together" — solved forever.

## Why it matters
- Directly answers two lines from the pain note: *"what I order doesn't go with anything"* and *"I don't know what to buy."*
- The closet is the **substrate** for almost every other Phase-2 idea:
  - [[Shop-Anywhere Companion]] → checks new items against what you own
  - [[Context & Mood Styling]] → builds looks from your closet first
  - [[Affiliate Marketplace]] → only recommends the *gaps*, not duplicates
- The more you add, the smarter and stickier it gets — a compounding data moat.

## How it could work
1. Populate the closet three ways: photos of what you own, purchases logged via [[Shop-Anywhere Companion]], and past ASTRA looks.
2. Auto-tag each piece (category, colour, silhouette, vibe) — same schema as [[Fashion Clothes Bank]].
3. Every recommendation is constrained to "works with your wardrobe."
4. Surface "cost-per-wear" and "orphan alerts" (a piece that goes with nothing).

## Open questions
- Lowest-friction way to add items (bulk photo → AI tag? import order emails?).
- Shared tagging schema with [[Fashion Clothes Bank]] from day one.
