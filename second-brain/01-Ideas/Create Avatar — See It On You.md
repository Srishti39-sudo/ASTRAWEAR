# Create Avatar — See It On You

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #create-avatar #glow-up #phase2

> **The instinct:** let her *SEE* it, not just read it. Right now the report *tells* her "define your waist" and she has to imagine it. Make the advice *visible on her own body* and the "will this actually work on me?" doubt disappears.

## Version A — Interactive Glow-Up (build NEXT, feasible now)
The buildable, soulful version — no 3D, uses the image we already generate from her own photo.

- **One page.** Show her glow-up self on the *same* page (no separate page 2).
- **Six focused cards only:** bust, neckline, waist, vertical proportion, legs, face type. That's the body-read — Create Avatar's real job.
- **Cut haircut + footwear** → those move to Personal Stylist.
- **Her generated image wears the new clothes** (from her own photo).
- **The magic — tap-to-highlight:** she taps the **waist** card → her waist region *lights up* on the image → *"here's your waist, and here's how we elongated it."* Abstract advice becomes "oh, I can see it." Same for bust / neckline / legs / vertical / face type.
- **Practical (v1):** we highlight *approximate zones* (face = top, bust = upper third, waist = middle, legs = lower, vertical = full height) with a soft glow. Not pixel-perfect, but more than enough for the emotional "that's my waist." Precise body-part segmentation = a later upgrade.

## Version B — Rotating 3D Body (post-funding DREAM)
A 3D rotating model of her body; tap a card → the model wears that piece so she can walk around it.

- **Honest reality:** one of the hardest things in fashion tech.
  - A 3D body that's actually *her* (face, skin, real proportions) is research-grade. A generic parametric mannequin would look like a video-game dummy — betrays the "this is really me" soul.
  - Real-time 3D garments draping on a custom body = a specialized discipline (CLO3D-level); every garment hand-modeled + rigged. Weeks–months, or an expensive SDK.
- **Verdict:** beautiful signature feature, wrong season. Needs a team / 3D SDK / funding. Park it; Version A delivers ~80% of the feeling now.

## Why it matters
Turns the report from *advice she has to trust* into *proof she can see.* Deepens the "she knows me / this is really me" feeling — the whole [[The Feeling — Loved, Not Sold]] core.
