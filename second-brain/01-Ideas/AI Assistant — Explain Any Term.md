# AI Assistant — Explain Any Term

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #assistant #teaching

## The idea
An in-app AI stylist assistant. If a user doesn't know a term (e.g. "soft draping", "empire waist", "cool undertone"), she taps it / asks, and ASTRA explains it warmly — in plain words, ideally with a quick example or picture.

## Why it matters
- Nobody should feel dumb or need a translator. Removes the one friction in a report full of styling language.
- It's the [[Style School — Learn Your Own Taste]] pillar made live — the app that *teaches* taste, not just hands it over.
- Turns a one-way report into a two-way relationship ("she can ask me anything").
- Great retention hook — a reason to come back and keep learning.

## How it could work
- Every styling term is subtly tappable → opens a short, warm explanation.
- Or a small "Ask ASTRA" chat on the results page.
- Powered by the same Gemini text model, with the woman's own profile as context ("for YOUR pear shape, soft draping means…").
- Keep the voice: warm, plain, no jargon — explain like a friend.

## When
Build at the END (after the core feature + launch). Noted so it's not forgotten.
