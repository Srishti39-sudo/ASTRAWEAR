# Context & Mood Styling

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #experience #astro

## The idea
Style for the *moment*, not just the mirror. Two modes:
- **Occasion**: "wedding Saturday / interview Monday / first date" → an instant look from your closet + the one or two pieces you're missing.
- **Mood + cosmic**: "dress me for confidence today," plus a daily astro style tip. Fashion as self-care, not a chore.

## Why it matters
- Real getting-dressed stress is **context-driven** — people freeze at *specific* moments, not in the abstract.
- Gives a reason to open ASTRA **daily** (mood/astro tip) and at **high-intent moments** (occasions) — covers both habit and value.
- The cosmic layer is a genuine differentiator no competitor has, and it's emotionally sticky.

## How it could work
1. **Occasion mode**: pick event + vibe → outfit from [[Wardrobe Memory]] first, gaps filled via [[Affiliate Marketplace]].
2. **Mood mode**: pick a feeling → a look tuned to it.
3. **Daily cosmic tip**: rotating style nudge from the astro engine (absorbs the old [[Daily Cosmic Style Tip]] idea).
4. Every occasion look is saveable to the [[Saved Looks Gallery]].
