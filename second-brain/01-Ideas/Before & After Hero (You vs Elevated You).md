# Before & After Hero (You → Elevated You)

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #experience #emotional

## The idea
On the glow-up page, instead of only showing the styled image, show a **subtle before/after**: her *current* photo beside her *elevated* self.

- Framed gently: **"You → Your elevated self"** (or "Current / Elevated").
- The line under it: *"This is how your own features look when styled intentionally."*
- NOT a dramatic makeover — same face, same body, just intentionally styled. The subtlety is the point.

## Why it matters
- Creates the huge **emotional moment** — "that's *me*, just loved and styled." Proof it's her, not a stranger.
- Reinforces the whole thesis: we don't replace you, we reveal you.
- Very shareable (before/after is the most viral content format).

## Needs
- The image model on (image-to-image keeping her exact face/body).
- A simple two-up layout with a soft divider / slider.
