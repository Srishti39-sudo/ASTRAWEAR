# Style School — Learn Your Own Taste

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #differentiator #retention

## The idea
ASTRA doesn't just hand you outfits — it **teaches you taste from scratch**. Every recommendation carries a one-line *why* ("this defines your waist because you're an hourglass"). Use it long enough and you graduate with your own sense of style.

> ASTRA is a stylist **and** a teacher. You leave with taste, not just a look.

## Why it matters
- Huge, unserved insight: **many people have no basic sense of fashion** and want to *build* it, not just be handed clothes. No competitor does this.
- Teaching = retention. People stay for *transformation and mastery*, not another outfit grid.
- It reframes the category: not "what to wear today" (utility) but "become someone with taste" (identity). Identity products don't churn.

## How it could work
1. Attach a short, plain-English rationale to every rec — tied to shape, palette, vibe, proportion.
2. A running "your style rules" page that fills in as ASTRA explains them — the user's personalised style handbook.
3. A visible **taste / confidence level** that climbs with use (see [[Glow-Up Guarantee]]).
4. Optional micro-lessons: "why necklines matter for your shape," 20 seconds each.

## Open questions
- How much explanation is delightful vs. too much? (Keep it one line by default, expandable.)
- Do we gamify the level, or keep it quiet and elegant (on-brand)?
