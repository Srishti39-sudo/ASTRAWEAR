# Learns You — The Anti-Quiz

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #personalization

## The idea
ASTRA learns your taste **without interrogating you**. Every competitor drowns the user in 30–50 questions. ASTRA reads you from your photo + a few taps, then keeps getting smarter *silently* from what you save, skip, and buy.

> The feeling we're selling: "it just *knows* me."

## Why it matters
- The #1 complaint about existing stylist apps: too many questions, and they still don't feel personal.
- Silent, behaviour-based learning = the **Endowment Effect** — the more the profile is *yours*, the less you'd ever switch. It becomes a switching-cost moat.
- Low friction at onboarding = far higher activation. Every question you remove saves users.

## How it could work
1. Seed the profile from the [[Body Scan & Avatar Input]] + a tiny taste signal (a few "this/not this" taps).
2. Log every implicit signal — saves, skips, dwell time, purchases — into an evolving taste profile.
3. Recommendations quietly re-rank over time. No re-quizzing, ever.
4. Occasionally confirm, don't ask: "you've been leaning minimal lately — lean in?"
