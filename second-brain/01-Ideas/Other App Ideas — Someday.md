# Other App Ideas — Someday

> Ideas beyond ASTRA. Caught here so they're safe — *not* to work on now. Stay on ASTRA; these wait.

## 1. The right song for your story (from trending)
An AI that reads the **emotional story of your clip** and picks the *perfect* trending song for it — not just a list of what's trending.

**The gap (checked Aug 2026):** tools exist that *track* trending audio (Trendy, Metricool, Dash Social) or *auto-attach* generic trending audio while editing (Alkai, Predis AI, SendShort, Freebeat). **None truly reads the mood/story of *your* specific content and says "this exact trending song is the one."** That precise match is still nobody's.

**Why it fits me:** same soul as Dress Your Reality — make people *feel* something. The "song that makes the moment land."

## 2. Curated gym app
A **personalized, curated** fitness app (not a generic workout dump). Seed only — to expand: what makes it *curated*? (your body + goals + what you actually enjoy → a plan that feels made for you, like ASTRA does for style). Ties to my own "design it around what you enjoy" formula.

## 3. Curated face / hair app
Personalized **face + hair** guidance — the ASTRA idea, but aimed specifically at the face (features, makeup, brows) and hair (cut, colour, part) that suit *you*. Could one day be an ASTRA extension or its own thing. Seed — expand later.
