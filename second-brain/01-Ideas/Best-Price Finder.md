# Best-Price Finder

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #money #shopping #agent

## The idea
Once ASTRA knows the *piece* you want, an AI agent scans **thousands of brands / retailers** and finds the **best price** for it (or the closest match). You get the right item — for the least money — without opening ten tabs.

> Seen in another app and loved it. Pairs perfectly with our styling: we decide *what* suits you, this finds *where* to get it cheapest.

## Why it matters
- Closes the loop from *"this suits you"* → *"here's the best place to buy it."* Turns advice into a purchase (and commission).
- Strong pull on human behaviour: **loss aversion** (nobody wants to overpay) + the little dopamine hit of "found it cheaper."
- Differentiator vs. Doji and try-on apps — they show clothes on you but don't *save you money*. Price is a universal motivator, even for people without fashion sense.
- Natural fit with [[Affiliate Marketplace]]: every "best price" link can be an affiliate link → revenue on the money-saving moment.

## How it could work
1. Take a target item (from a recommendation, a [[Shop-Anywhere Companion]] link, or a photo).
2. Identify it / find visually-similar matches across retailers (product feeds + image match).
3. Rank by price (also flag: in stock, your size, shipping, dupe vs. exact).
4. Return "best price" + 2–3 alternatives, each a buy link.
5. Optional: **price-drop alerts** on saved items (reason to keep the app; loss-aversion again).

## Watch-outs
- Data source: affiliate/product feeds are cleaner than scraping (and scraping many retail sites has legal/ToS limits — prefer official feeds & APIs).
- "Best price" must stay honest, not just "highest commission" — trust is the whole game.
- Exact-match vs. look-alike dupe: label clearly.

## Open questions
