# The Feeling — Loved, Not Sold

Status: core principle · Tags: #principle #brand #voice #experience

## The north star (from the founder, after sitting at her own stylist)
Every user should feel:
- **Loved and warm** — like someone poured genuine care into them, asking nothing in return.
- **Seen** — "this is really me, and I'm beautiful."
- The reaction we're chasing: *"OMG I can't believe the result is this beautiful — god bless whoever made this app for me."*

## What this means for every choice
- **Voice:** warm, generous, tender — a loving stylist/friend, never a clinical app or a salesy tool. Affirm, never grade.
- **It's a gift, not a funnel:** no pushy asks, no "upgrade to see", no cold forms. It should feel like someone did this *for* her.
- **The reveal is a love letter:** the report and the glow-up should feel like hours of care spent on her because she's worth it.
- **Words:** describe her by her beauty and strengths (the tag rule); microcopy — loading lines, headings, the reveal — should feel held and cherished.

## Litmus test for any screen or line of copy
Would a woman reading this feel **cared for and beautiful** — or **processed**? If processed, rewrite it warmer.
