# Color Analysis — Face Scan

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #personalization #color

## The idea
Scan the person's **face** (from their photo) to run **colour analysis** — read their undertone and hand back the palette that actually flatters them: their season, best whites/blacks/neutrals, gold vs. silver, and makeup colours.

> The third pillar of "know yourself": body ✅, face ✅, **colour** ← this.

## Why it matters
- Completes the analysis trio. Right now the report does **body + face**; colour is the missing layer real stylists always cover.
- Colour is the single biggest "why do I look tired / washed out?" fix — high-impact, very shareable ("your season is Soft Autumn" is a viral format).
- Feeds everything downstream:
  - The [[Styling Pipeline]] → palette constrains every recommendation
  - [[Best-Price Finder]] / [[Affiliate Marketplace]] → filter shopping to *your* colours
  - Ties beautifully to the astro palette (two palettes → one signature)

## How it could work
1. Use the **same uploaded photo** — no extra step. Gemini (multimodal) reads skin/hair/eye tone.
2. Return: undertone (warm/cool/neutral), seasonal palette (e.g. Soft Summer), best neutrals, metal (gold/silver), 3–4 hero colours, makeup direction.
3. Add a small **colour-swatch strip** to the report (visual, screenshot-worthy).
4. Later: reconcile with the birth-chart palette into one "signature palette."

## Watch-outs
- Photo lighting skews colour badly — nudge for natural light / no heavy filter; state confidence.
- Keep it plain: "you glow in warm ivory, not stark white" — not colour-theory jargon.

## Open questions
- Trust a single photo, or ask for a well-lit selfie for colour specifically?
- Show as swatches only, or swatches + one-line why?
