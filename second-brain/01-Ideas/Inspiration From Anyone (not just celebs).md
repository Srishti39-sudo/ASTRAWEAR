# Inspiration From Anyone (not just celebs)

Status: seed · Tags: #idea #phase2 #questions

## The idea
In the identity questions, let the user name **anyone whose presence inspires them** — a friend, a relative, an icon — not only celebrities. "Presence" (posture, confidence, attitude, style) is richer than a famous face.

## Why it parked
Right now Q2 is **celebrity-only**, because Gemini knows how a public figure carries herself but can't look up a private person. A friend's energy can't be translated without more input.

## How it could work later
- If they name a non-celebrity, follow up: "describe their energy in a few words" or "upload a photo of them" → feed that description/image into the analysis instead of relying on the model's knowledge.
- Or offer a small set of "energy archetypes" to pick from if they can't name anyone.
