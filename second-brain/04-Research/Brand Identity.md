# Brand Identity — name, font, symbol

Tags: #brand #ui
Direction: **old money · Morocco · clean, minimal, trustworthy**

## The diagnosis (why Saint Laurent & Hermès "speak money")
- **The logo is small, not big.** Confidence is restraint. Power comes from the typeface, not the size.
- **Photography does the talking.** ~90% full-bleed image, ~10% tiny text. Our page was 90% empty beige + effects → that's the "AI" tell.
- **Zero effects.** No 3D, no gradients, no script fonts, no sparkles.
- → Implication: **drop the 3D mystery box + carousel.** Luxury houses never use gimmicks. See [[3D Mystery Box]] (retire).

## Decisions to lock
- [ ] **Name** — `ASTRA` (maison) vs `ASTRAWEAR` (app). Recommendation: brand = ASTRA, domain can stay astrawear.
- [ ] **Font** — options: Archivo letterspaced caps (SL/modern) · Marcellus roman caps (Hermès/heritage) · Italiana (editorial thin) · Cormorant Garamond (soft timeless)
- [ ] **Symbol** — ⭐ recommended: **eight-pointed star** (astrological + Moroccan zellige motif)

Recommended combo: **ASTRA · Marcellus caps · eight-point star above.**

## The brand story (locked idea)
> **Hermès:** we give you the carriage, *you take the reins.*
> **ASTRA:** we give you the stylist, *you take flight.*

## ✅ LOGO — DECIDED
**Two swans coming together to give you wings.** Two swans, necks curving into a **heart**, wings spread wide.
- Meaning: two souls / you + your best self meeting → and the **wings** they give you. The ugly-duckling-becomes-a-swan transformation = the product's promise.
- Swans are **Aphrodite's bird** too → keeps the love + beauty + classical-Europe thread.
- Style: **fine line / engraved**, one colour (near-black `#33302A`), minimal, old money yet cool. The **heart is the negative space** between the necks — never drawn.
- Wordmark: **ASTRA** in a refined serif (Cinzel / Marcellus), wide letter-spacing.
- Variants needed: full mark, white version (for the dark video hero), sealed-in-ring favicon.
- Status: **concept locked, artwork later.** Design it myself in Figma/Canva → see [[Design the Winged ASTRA Logo]]. Send the PNG/SVG to Claude to drop into the top bar.
- ⚠️ Final logo needs proper vector refinement — draw my own so I own it for trademark.

## Why the symbol matters
A symbol is what makes the Hermès-style **loading animation** possible. With the star, the loader = the star drawing itself / slowly rotating. It also becomes the favicon and future app icon.
